Hatlem roots in Hyllestad

Hatlem is a farm area in Hyllestad, Sogn: Indre Hatlem (gnr. 26) and Ytre Hatlem (gnr. 27). In 1900 several families lived there, e.g. Mads Pedersen (b. 1856) & Britha Endresdatter (b. 1863) at Indre Hatlem bnr. 2. Which family Martha descends from is not yet known. 1900 census, Hatlem · Indre Hatlem bnr. 2 · Hyllestad bygdebok vol. II, pp. 196–219 (index)

Olsen / Stub roots

In 1910 Elise Olsen (b. 29 Dec 1864, Ålesund), widow, lived at Smedviken in Kabelvåg with her children Arne, Aslaug and Trygve. In 1891 an Elise Marie Stub (b. 1864) lived at Smedvigen with Karen Stub (b. 1834) and Kaia Stub (b. 1863). In 1865 Elise Marie Stub, 2, lived in Ålesund with her parents Johan J. B. Stub and Karen Marie Hansdatter and her siblings Hans C. and Kaia. The matching birth year and place, the place she lived and Trygve's surname "Stub-Olsen" make it likely she was born a Stub. A baptism or marriage record would confirm it. Arne Robert's father (an Olsen, d. before 1910) has not been found yet. 1910 · 1891 · 1865

Hatlem / Henneli

Harald Ingolf Hatlem was born on 11 Jul 1904 at Hatlem in Hyllestad. He was the son of Hans and Ingeborg Hatlem, both born in Hyllestad in 1868. The family moved to Våle in Vestfold (1910) and then to Nesttun in Fana (1920), where Harald was an apprentice typographer. On 5 May 1944 he married Klara Cecilie Henneli (b. 1914) at Birkeland in Fana. Klara Cecilie was born on 16 Jul 1914, and a girl named Klara Cecilie born on that date was baptised at Birkeland on 27 Sep 1914. Her parents are listed as Lars Larsen (b. 1871) and Marta Paulsdatter (b. 1877) of Henne. The matching date, place and surname make this very likely her, the family has confirmed it.

In 1900 Hans Hansen "jun." (b. 1868) owned Ytre Hatlem (gnr. 27, bnr. 7) in Hyllestad. Hans Hansen "sen." (b. 1838) and Petrine Andersdatter (b. 1842) lived with him as retired farmers (føderaad). That is very likely his father and mother, but the census doesn't state the relationship, so they are shown as probable. Ingeborg Hansdatter's parents and Lars Larsen's parents have not been found yet. Marta's farm name, Nøttveit, comes from the 1920 census.

More names, birth years and places for the grandparents will let the tree go back further.
