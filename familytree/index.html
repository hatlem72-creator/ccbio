Research notes

Public records searched so far have not linked older generations with confidence. These are leads only, not part of the tree:

  • An Arne Robert Olsen, b. 1899 in Kabelvåg (Lofoten), lived in Bergen in 1920 and 1934 — same name, connection unknown. 1920 census, 1934 register
  • A Hatlem family lived in Bergen in 1917 (Bernt Andersen Hatlem, b. 1856). Connection unknown. 1917 census
  • The Hatlem name comes from farms in Hyllestad, Sogn (e.g. Indre Hatlem). Brudvik genealogy

More names, birth years and places for the grandparents will let the tree go back further.
